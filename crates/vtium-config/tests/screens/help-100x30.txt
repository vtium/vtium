 The Flow view: CONFIGURATION, NEW PATH and PARTS
   ↑          Moves to the box above.
   ↓          Moves to the box below.
   ←          Moves to the box before, and on to the line before.
   →          Moves to the box after, and on to the next line.
   Home       Goes to the first box.
   End        Goes to the last box.
   PgUp       Scrolls up by a window.
   PgDn       Scrolls down by a window.
   Space      Picks the highlighted part into NEW PATH, or takes it out.
   Enter      Opens the highlighted box: a part's detail, a path's card, a template's part maker, or
              the list of other inputs.
   1          Goes straight to the Fonts tab.
   3          Goes straight to the Looks tab.
   5          Goes straight to the Settings tab.
   6          Goes straight to the Help tab.
   s          Saves the changes that are not saved into the draft.
   i          Saves, then asks whether to install the draft.
   r          Asks whether to throw away the draft's changes and copy the installed file back.
   n          Asks whether to start a new configuration.
   v          Shows the draft's vtium.conf as text.
   o          Lists the files installed before, newest first, to copy one into the draft.
   .          Shows the key line's next page, and the first page again after the last. Does nothing
              where the keys fit on one row.
