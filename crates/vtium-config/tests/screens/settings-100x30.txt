 Enter turns a switch on or off, steps a choice, or types a value; an empty value is the default.
 The font vtium draws text with. It is a frit's name, `NAME.vtfrit` in `/etc/vtium/fonts`, whose
 glyphs the helper generates at run time. A kbd font's name, or with `font-engine=psf` a PSF font's
 path, that no frit bears becomes a one-source frit. Unset, vconsole's font; with none, or a name
 that is neither, vtium draws its built-in fonts and logs the name and why.
