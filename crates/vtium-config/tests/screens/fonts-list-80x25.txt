 Space activates or deactivates the highlighted font. Enter opens a font's
 detail, or the filters on the filter line. p switches the preview to the

 This terminal does not support images, so here is a written description
 instead. With this font and size, each character cell is 8x16 pixels, the
 console will be 80x30 characters, and the font can display 40 of the 51
 characters in the preview. Superscripts and subscripts are this font's own
 characters, scaled to two thirds of their size. Each pixel stays fully on or
 fully off, so the edges stay sharp.
