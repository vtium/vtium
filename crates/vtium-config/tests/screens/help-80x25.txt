 The Fonts tab: a line of filters, then the fonts found on this system, each
 with its active mark and trust mark, with a preview of the highlighted font
   ↑          Moves to the item above.
   ↓          Moves to the item below.
   Home       Goes to the first item.
   End        Goes to the last item.
   PgUp       Moves up by a page.
   PgDn       Moves down by a page.
   Space      On a font, a color map, a shader, a color scheme file or a pointer
              theme, activates it, or deactivates it when it is active. A
              pointer theme is activated by its folder, with every file in it.
              Deactivating asks first when a look uses it. On a vtlook, the
              built-in font or a built-in color scheme, does nothing.
   Enter      On the filter line, shows the filters as a list. On a font, opens
              its detail: its files, what it covers, its sizes, its trust mark
              and the looks that use it.
   2          Goes straight to the Effects tab.
   3          Goes straight to the Looks tab.
   4          Goes straight to the Flow tab.
