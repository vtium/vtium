 How many character cells a syllable takes when it has a spacing vowel sign or a virama that joins
 two consonants, in Tamil, Devanagari and other scripts of South and Southeast Asia. `double`, the
 default, gives such a syllable two cells, so கொ  takes two. `single` gives it one cell and squeezes
 it to fit. For example, the Tamil syllable கொ  is the letter க with the vowel sign ொ . Under …
