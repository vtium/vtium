# systemd, logind and PID 1

vtium runs without systemd: launched by its unit, an init script or by hand (ADR-1400). This page lists every place vtium touches systemd, logind or the conventions of a systemd PID 1, and what it does on a host without them. A touchpoint not listed here is a defect in this page.

"Without systemd" is a host whose PID 1 is another init (sysvinit, OpenRC, runit, s6, busybox) with no logind. "elogind" is such a host running elogind, the standalone logind, which owns `org.freedesktop.login1` on the system bus and ships libelogind in place of libsystemd; vtium's `logind` feature links whichever of the two pkg-config finds, libsystemd first.

## Touchpoints

| Touchpoint | Where | With systemd | Without systemd | Under elogind | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The VT unit, `kmsconvt@.service`, aliased `autovt@.service` | `crates/vtium/install/kmsconvt@.service`, installed by `make install-tree` | logind starts `autovt@ttyN` for each VT it spawns, which is vtium in place of getty, with getty's ordering and TTY settings (`TTYReset`, `TTYVHangup`, `TTYVTDisallocate`), `Conflicts=getty@%i` and no sandboxing directive (ADR-1400) | The unit is unused. An inittab line, an init script or a supervisor runs `vtium --vt=ttyN --no-switchvt` where it ran a getty, and the TTY reset and hangup are the init's to do. vtium then starts at the boot and waits for its VT to be shown (tier 3's `sysvinit-640x480`). Two such lines start two vtiums at once, which race for DRM master and one loses (vtium/vtium#139; tier 3's `sysvinit-two-vts`) | As without systemd | none |

| The log | `crates/vtium/src/logger.rs`: one line a message on stderr | The unit sends stderr to the journal | stderr goes wherever the init points it, such as the console or a log file | As without systemd | none |

With no udev daemon, the scan still finds the devices in sysfs, but none carries `ID_SEAT` (each is taken as `seat0`) or `ID_PATH` (a `mode=` line naming a card matches none), and no hotplug arrives; vtium draws and reads its keyboard (tier 3's `sysvinit-no-udev`) | As without systemd | none |

| `grab-reboot` at `kernel.ctrl-alt-del` 0 | `crates/vtium-platform/src/power.rs` (`interrupt_init`), `crates/vtium/src/power.rs` | `SIGINT` to PID 1, which systemd takes as `ctrl-alt-del.target`, a reboot that stops the services and unmounts the filesystems | `SIGINT` to PID 1 is the kernel's own Ctrl+Alt+Del convention: sysvinit runs its inittab `ctrlaltdel` entry, `shutdown -r`, which stops the services in runlevel 6 before the restart (tier 3's `reboot-sysvinit`); busybox init, runit and s6 have their own (not tested by vtium) | As without systemd | none |
| `grab-reboot` at any other `kernel.ctrl-alt-del` | the same, `restart_kernel` | `sync()` and `reboot(RB_AUTOBOOT)`: the kernel restarts at once, with no shutdown by systemd | The same; no init is involved (tier 3's `sysvinit-restart`) | The same | none |
| `grab-power-off` | `crates/vtium-platform/src/power.rs` (`power_off_init`), with libc's `SIGRTMIN` | `SIGRTMIN+4` to PID 1, which systemd takes as `poweroff.target`: services stopped, filesystems unmounted, power off | sysvinit catches the signal too and powers off: runlevel 0, the services stopped, then power off (tier 3's `power-off-sysvinit`). An init that does not catch it, such as busybox init, never sees it: the kernel drops a signal PID 1 does not catch, and nothing powers off (not tested by vtium; vtium/vtium#140) | As without systemd | none |
| Blank and wake from outside the session | `crates/vtium/src/blank.rs`, `crates/vtium-platform/src/rt_signal.rs` | `SIGRTMIN+1` blanks the displays and `SIGRTMIN+2` wakes them; `systemctl kill --kill-whom=main -s SIGRTMIN+1 kmsconvt@ttyN` sends one to the vtium on VT N alone, where without `--kill-whom=main` the login session's programs get it too and end | `vtium blank --vt N` and `vtium wake --vt N` find the vtium on VT N and signal it | As without systemd | none |
| Whether PID 1 catches the power-off signal | `crates/vtium/src/check.rs` (`semantics`) | The `SigCgt` mask in `/proc/1/status` has `SIGRTMIN+4`'s bit, and no warning is given (tier 3's `power-off`) | The same under sysvinit (tier 3's `power-off-sysvinit`). Where PID 1 does not catch it, a bound `grab-power-off` warns at start and in `check-config` that it powers nothing off, and where `/proc/1/status` cannot be read or holds no mask, that the answer is unknown | As without systemd | none |
| `grab-suspend` | `crates/vtium-platform/src/power.rs` (`suspend`), `src/logind.rs` | Built with `logind`: logind's `Suspend` over the system bus through sd-bus, answered within 2 s, so logind's delay inhibitors hold the sleep and its `PrepareForSleep` signal runs other sessions' lock-on-suspend. A refusal is reported and never worked around. Built without `logind` (minspec): as without systemd | No logind on the bus, or a bus error that it cannot be started (masked, failing to spawn): `mem` written to `/sys/power/state` as root by a shell vtium starts with no environment, so the loop serves the kernel's switch to its suspend console. No inhibitor holds the sleep and no `PrepareForSleep` is sent; a failed write is logged as an error | elogind owns `org.freedesktop.login1`, so a build with `logind` reaches its `Suspend` through libelogind's sd-bus, or libsystemd's where both are installed; the minspec build writes `/sys/power/state` | `logind`, sd-bus from libsystemd or libelogind; off in minspec |

## What vtium never does

- It reads none of logind's state files under `/run/systemd`; ADR-1400 rejected them as an interface. What PID 1 is, it never asks; it reads only which signals PID 1 catches.
- It links no libsystemd or libelogind in the minspec build: `ldd` on it lists neither (ADR-1400's observable).
- It sends PID 1 no other signal, and calls `reboot(2)` only for `grab-reboot` at a non-zero `kernel.ctrl-alt-del`.

## Where it is tested

Tier 3 runs two guests (`tools/tier3/README.md`). The Arch Linux guest runs systemd and logind; its power variants prove the reboot paths, power-off, and suspend through logind and through `/sys/power/state`. The guest without systemd, Debian with sysvinit as PID 1 and no logind (`make tier3-nosystemd`), proves this page's column for a host without systemd: vtium started by an inittab line and drawing, with the udev daemon and without it, the reboot through the inittab's `ctrlaltdel` entry and the kernel's own, and the power-off through sysvinit. Each row it proves names its variant or case. Untested still: busybox init, runit and s6, an init that ignores `SIGRTMIN+4` (vtium/vtium#140), and elogind (vtium/vtium#100). The unit tests stand in for PID 1, logind and the kernel (`crates/vtium/src/power.rs`, `crates/vtium/tests/power_keys.rs`).
