# tier 2's goldens

The goldens of tier 2's screens. Each judges vtium. Most were first drawn by a program that is retired now (vtium/vtium#111) and are kept as they were drawn. vtium drew the outline goldens from the test font set of outline fonts (ADR-1503 D9), the two `osd-` goldens without a size in their names (vtium/vtium#169), the overlay goldens whose names end in `-800x480` or `-3840x540` (ADR-1401 D5, D7), the two `small-` goldens (vtium/vtium#75), the two `vitrified-` goldens, from sets built as `vtium-config create` builds them (vtium/vtium#126), `emoji-rgba` (vtium/vtium#23), `emoji-rgba-marks` (ADR-1302 D5), the two `link-hover-` goldens and the three `link-overlay-` goldens (ADR-1002), behaviour only vtium has. A golden added here stays in `../provisional.tsv` until the operator has reviewed it.

On a double-width line or a double-height pair, vtium cuts each glyph's ink at the line's top and bottom edges. A glyph whose ink reaches past its character cell therefore loses those rows on a double-size line, though at normal size the row above or below draws them. The goldens `lines-emoji`, `lines-marks`, `lines-arabic`, `lines-indic`, `lines-graphics`, `lines-graphics-unifont` and `lines-powerline` show that cut, and the maintainer accepted them with it on 2026-11-15. For example, `lines-marks` loses the top of Å's ring and of Ệ's marks. The cut is expected for now. [The double-size ink issue](https://github.com/vtium/vtium/issues/417) tracks letting that ink draw past the line, and that change would move these goldens in a golden commit of its own.
