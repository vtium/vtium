# vtium's goldens

The goldens of vtium's own tier 2 screens, which the C reference's tier 2 has none for. Each is reviewed vtium output that judges vtium. The C reference drew every one but the two `small-` goldens and `emoji-rgba`, with a program built from kmscon `b1f7a7c5df563f3378584db7298753848a7a9a13` and libtsm `3ec40dc7a185dea1cd7e1252d4215939af1977fb`, the commit that kmscon's `subprojects/libtsm.wrap` pinned, through libtsm, bbulk with the unifont backend and drm2d's blend; that program is retired (vtium/vtium#111). vtium drew the two `small-` goldens (vtium/vtium#75) and `emoji-rgba` (vtium/vtium#23), behaviour only vtium has. A golden added here stays in `../provisional.tsv` until the operator has reviewed it.

| Golden | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `edges.ppm` | 93x67 pixels: a grid of 12 by 4 cells, of which the display holds 11 columns and 4 rows, centred with 2 and 3 pixel columns of border at the left and right and 1 and 2 pixel rows at the top and bottom. `edges` with red `R` and green `G` cells; a blue row; `last456`, `last` black on yellow and `456` underlined and struck; a magenta run in the last row. In the last whole column, the left halves of an underlined `中` in row 1 and a reversed `語` in row 3, with a default-background cell between them. |
| `edges-right.ppm` | The same on a display turned right, 67x93: text reads top to bottom. |
| `edges-upside-down.ppm` | The same turned half round. The two cut wide glyphs show their right halves: the C reference's aaronsb/kmscon#44, which vtium departs from. |
| `edges-left.ppm` | The same turned left: text reads bottom to top. The cut wide glyphs show their right halves, as in `edges-upside-down`. |
| `erase-pen.ppm` | 320x96: a red top row scrolled in, then `line one stays as it is`; `line two is ` and red to the end; two red cells and `line three gets two cells inserted`; `line four stays as it is`; `line five is erase` and red to the end. No red cell carries an underline or a strike. |
| `vs16-unifont.ppm` | 256x80: `a`, the heart and `b` four times. The VS16 heart and the underlined VS16 heart take two cells, the text heart centred in them; the plain and VS15 hearts take one, cut to it. |
| `vs16-wcwidth.ppm` | The same by wcwidth: every heart one cell, the VS16 ones included. |
| `wide-deco-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `日本語` underlined, `漢字` curly, `カタカナ` dotted in red; `한글` double, `中文` dashed, `日本語` struck; `a日b本c` underlined; and `語` underlined and struck at the start of row 6, wrapped from the last column of row 5. |
| `redraw-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `unchanged line`, `line 2 CHANGED curly` with `curly` under a curly line, `line three stays`, and `new struck` struck through. |
| `ascii-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: the pangram in both cases, digits and punctuation, runs of `i`, `l`, `W` and `m`, and a line of code, each glyph in its own 8x16 cell. |
| `bold-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `bold` beside `regular`, a bold pangram over two lines, and bold red, green and blue words; bold glyphs widened by a pixel. |
| `palette-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: the 16 background colors as two-cell blocks, `AB` in each of the 16 foreground colors, a 19-step ramp through the 256-color palette, and a 9-step red-to-green gradient. |
| `inverse-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `inverse` in reverse between plain words, a whole line reversed, `swapped` green on red after red-on-green text, and `bold inverse`. |
| `cjk-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: Japanese, Chinese and Korean in two-cell glyphs, full-width Latin, `a日b本c語d`, and wide text in bold and in reverse. |
| `box-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: light and double boxes around `cell`, shades and blocks, eighth blocks, rounded, heavy and diagonal pieces, joined from cell to cell. |
| `underline-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `gjpqy` under single, double, curly, dotted and dashed lines, each in the text color and in an SGR 58 color; a yellow curl under red text that SGR 59 returns to red; underlines on blue, in reverse and in bold. |
| `strike-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: struck text alone, in red, on green, with a straight and a curly underline, in bold, in reverse, and six struck spaces. |
| `deco-config-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: an underline in index 196's red and a strike in `#00c0ff`; SGR 58's green curl and yellow dots in their own colors; green underlined and yellow struck text; a double underline and strike in the configured colors. |
| `deco-force-unifont.ppm` | The same with `force-decoration-colors`: SGR 58's curl and dots drawn in the configured red too. |
| `combining-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: `é ã ñ` precomposed; the decomposed forms drawn as their base letters alone, in words, underlined, struck and bold, since unifont draws a cluster's first character. |
| `mosaic-unifont.ppm` | 576x256: every Braille pattern, sextant and octant with their labels, then sextant and octant checkerboards, a Braille grid and wave, sextant and octant bands, and the checkerboards and grid in yellow on blue. |
| `emoji-unifont.ppm` | 384x160: each emoji as its unifont glyph in grey, the family, tone, flag and keycap as their first characters, the hearts, an emoji between letters and under each decoration, the Unicode 15 codepoint, and the grin wrapped from the last column to row 8. |
| `emoji-rgba.ppm` | 384x160, a set built in memory: unifont's ASCII in 1 bit and a grin, a rocket and a heart in RGBA, each two cells wide. `a`, grin, `b`, rocket, `c`, VS16 heart, `d` and a bold grin, each glyph in its own colors; the glyphs on blue, on yellow and on green, with the rocket's translucent orange flame tinted by each and the glyphs' edges blended into it; reversed on the light grey of the default foreground, and a grin on blue from blue on yellow; a white underline beneath three glyphs, a red curl beneath two, a double line on blue and a green line beneath the rocket, each showing where the glyph is clear and hidden where it is opaque; a white strike across three glyphs, a red one, a white strike and underline on red, and a black strike across a reversed grin, each over the glyph; `a❤b` with no selector and with VS15, the heart cut to its middle cell, then with VS16 two cells wide, and the cut heart on red; `lacks ?,` with the missing emoji as `?` centred in two cells, `green` in green and `bold blue` bold; and the grin wrapped from the last column of row 8 to the start of row 9. |
| `small-320x240.ppm` | 320x240, the 8x8 PSF2 test font, 40 by 30 cells. Row 1 a reversed title to the last column; `login: pi`; a green and blue prompt and `ls`; `bin` bold blue, `run.sh` bold green, `notes.txt`, `lib.so` cyan; a prompt and `echo` whose last letter wraps to row 6; the echoed line; a two-by-two box of sprite lines around `bold` in bold, `under` underlined, `struck` struck and `reverse` reversed; the 16 background colors, two cells each; `AB` in the 16 foreground colors, the first black on white; shades, halves, eighths, Braille and sextants as sprites; Latin-1 letters and signs; quotes, dashes, the ellipsis, bullet, euro sign and arrows; `lacks:` with `中` as `?` in two cells and the snowman as `?` in one; `curly`, `double`, `dots` and `dash` underlined and `red` under a red line; `curl` under a red line, `double` under green, `dots` under yellow and `dash` under magenta, `struck` red and struck, and `both` cyan, struck and over a yellow line; a prompt and `vi notes.txt`; `right edge` in green ending in column 40 of row 28; an editor's status line reversed across row 30. At 8 pixels every underline lies in the cell's bottom row, the row below the letters: the double and the curl draw as the single line, and the dashed line meets the letters' bottom row. |
| `small-640x480.ppm` | 640x480, the 8x16 PSF1 test font, 80 by 30 cells: the title reversed across row 1; `login: pi`; `ls -l` with `bin` bold blue and `run.sh` bold green; an `echo` whose last word wraps to row 9 and its line; a light box of four cells around bold, underlined, struck and reversed words, and double, heavy and rounded boxes around `ital` upright, `dim` in grey and `blink` as plain text; the 16 background colors and `AB` in the 16 foregrounds on one row; a 256-color ramp and a 24-bit red-to-green ramp; shades, blocks, eighths, Braille, sextants and quadrants; Latin-1 with accents; quotes, dashes, the euro sign, arrows and U+FFFD; `中文` as two `?` in two cells each, the snowman and the note as `?`, and `a` with a combining acute as `a`; the five underline styles, a red curl, red struck text and bold underlined text; `green under`, `yellow double`, `magenta dots` and `cyan dash` under lines of those colors, `green strike` green and struck, and `blue, red curl` blue, struck and over a red curl; a prompt and `vi notes.txt`; `right edge` ending in column 80 of row 28; the status line reversed across row 30. |

`edges-upside-down` and `edges-left` differ from what vtium draws at the two cut wide glyphs only; `../departures.tsv` lists those cells under the render crate's departure.
